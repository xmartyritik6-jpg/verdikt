Best phones between ₹25,000 and ₹45,000

10 phones · ₹26,999 – ₹44,999 · ranked by Trust Score · updated 2026-07-29

This is where most people actually shop, and where the marketing gets loudest. Every phone here claims a flagship-grade something — a 200MP camera, a 7,000mAh battery, a 144Hz screen. The number is usually real. What it does for you in daily use is the part the ad leaves out.

Ranked by Trust Score below. Pay particular attention to software support length: at this price a phone should still be getting security patches three or four years from now, and not all of these will be.

  1. 1
    Samsung Galaxy S24 FE
    ₹39,999 · Trust Score 80/100

    The Galaxy S24 FE brings flagship-adjacent specs — a bright 120Hz display, seven years of promised updates, and IP68 protection — at a price that has dropped significantly from launch.

    The trade-off: Non-LTPO panel means the display can't drop refresh rate as aggressively, costing some battery efficiency.

  2. 2
    OnePlus 13R 5G
    ₹37,943 · Trust Score 79/100

    13R brings genuine near-flagship speed — Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, a bright LTPO AMOLED display, and 80W charging — while skipping extras like wireless charging to hit a lower price.

    The trade-off: Some users report network connectivity issues (dropping to airplane mode, requiring restarts) after a recent software update.

  3. 3
    OnePlus Nord 6 5G
    ₹43,990 · Trust Score 78/100

    Nord 6 leads with a genuinely massive 9,000mAh battery — unusual even by 2026 standards — paired with a fast 165Hz display and comprehensive IP66/68/69/69K durability.

    The trade-off: Price has been hiked three times since launch per reported coverage, eroding its original value-for-money positioning.

  4. 4
    Realme 15 Pro 5G
    ₹30,399 · Trust Score 76/100

    Realme 15 Pro packs a genuinely huge 7,000mAh battery and a bright 144Hz AMOLED display into a mid-range price, making it a strong pick for anyone anxious about battery life.

    The trade-off: Dropped the telephoto lens some users expected at this price, relying on digital zoom instead.

  5. 5
    Vivo V50 5G
    ₹34,999 · Trust Score 75/100

    V50 leans on its ZEISS-branded 50MP dual camera system and a genuinely large curved AMOLED display, positioned as a camera-first mid-ranger under ₹40,000.

    The trade-off: Lens aperture has stayed largely unchanged across recent V-series generations, per user feedback, limiting low-light improvement.

  6. 6
    Xiaomi Redmi Note 15 Pro+ 5G
    ₹37,999 · Trust Score 75/100

    Note 15 Pro+ brings a 200MP main camera and genuinely rugged IP66/68/69 protection (reviewers cite it surviving ice-submersion and drop tests) to the upper-mid-range.

    The trade-off: No telephoto lens despite the price, a recurring complaint across multiple reviews.

  7. 7
    OPPO Reno 14 5G
    ₹38,230 · Trust Score 74/100

    Reno 14 5G pairs a genuinely durable IP69-rated aerospace-grade aluminum frame with a capable triple-camera setup including a 50MP telephoto lens — rare at this price.

    The trade-off: A recent ColorOS 16 update introduced reported bugs — camera shutter delay, slower lens switching, and faster battery drain.

  8. 8
    Samsung Galaxy A56 5G
    ₹41,990 · Trust Score 74/100

    The Galaxy A56 5G plays it safe — a solid AMOLED display, dependable Exynos 1580 chipset, and genuine IP67 protection make it a reliable everyday phone.

    The trade-off: Exynos 1580 chipset falls behind rival chipsets in raw gaming performance at a similar price.

  9. 9
    Apple iPhone 13
    ₹44,999 · Trust Score 73/100

    iPhone 13 remains a strong value pick years after launch — the A15 Bionic chip still outperforms most Android phones in this price range, and MagSafe keeps it compatible with newer accessories.

    The trade-off: Lightning port instead of USB-C limits fast charging and cable compatibility with newer devices.

  10. 10
    Xiaomi Redmi Note 15 5G
    ₹26,999 · Trust Score 71/100

    The standard Note 15 trims the Pro+'s camera and charging speed but keeps a genuinely long-lasting battery, positioning it as the value pick in Xiaomi's latest Note lineup.

    The trade-off: Camera resolution and charging speed both trail the Pro+ and Pro variants in the same lineup.

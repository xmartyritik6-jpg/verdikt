Best phones above ₹45,000

3 phones · ₹48,838 – ₹82,900 · ranked by Trust Score · updated 2026-07-28

Above ₹45,000 raw specification stops being the deciding factor. Every phone here is fast enough for anything you will do with it. What you are paying for is camera processing, build materials, how long the software stays supported, and resale value — things that don't fit neatly in a spec sheet.

Ranked by Trust Score. The gap between the phones here is smaller than the gap in price, so be honest with yourself about which of these differences you will actually notice.

  1. 1
    Apple iPhone 16
    ₹64,900 · Trust Score 82/100

    The iPhone 16 brings the A18 chip and a genuinely useful Camera Control button, and its price has settled meaningfully below launch now that the iPhone 17 is the current flagship.

    The trade-off: Still limited to a 60Hz display, unlike the 120Hz ProMotion screen on the iPhone 17.

  2. 2
    Apple iPhone 17
    ₹82,900 · Trust Score 79/100

    The base iPhone 17 finally gets features that used to be Pro-only — a 120Hz ProMotion display and the same A19 chip as the Pro models — making it the most sensible iPhone to buy this year.

    The trade-off: Starting price of ₹82,900 puts it well above budget and even most mid-range Android flagships.

  3. 3
    OPPO Reno 14 Pro 5G
    ₹48,838 · Trust Score 77/100

    Reno 14 Pro steps up the design and camera experience over the standard Reno 14, with reviewers praising its display sharpness and AI feature set.

    The trade-off: Reviewers note the phone tries to balance too many things at once, leaving some features feeling not fully polished.

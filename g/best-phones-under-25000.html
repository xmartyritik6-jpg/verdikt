Best phones under ₹25,000

5 phones · ₹13,899 – ₹23,999 · ranked by Trust Score · updated 2026-07-29

Under ₹25,000 you are choosing which compromise you can live with, not whether there will be one. Nearly every phone in this band gives you a bright AMOLED panel and a battery that lasts the day; what varies is the chipset, how long the brand keeps updating it, and how the phone holds up after a year.

The list below is ordered by Trust Score — our read on how well each phone delivers what it advertises, weighed against its price. Read the trade-off line under each one before you decide, because at this price the weakness matters more than the headline spec.

  1. 1
    OnePlus Nord CE4 5G
    ₹23,999 · Trust Score 78/100

    The Nord CE4 pairs a Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 chipset with genuinely fast 100W charging, going from empty to full in well under 30 minutes — a rare combination at this price.

    The trade-off: Low-light photos show visible noise and softness compared to pricier OnePlus models.

  2. 2
    Samsung Galaxy M35 5G
    ₹21,999 · Trust Score 75/100

    The Galaxy M35 5G packs genuinely premium-feeling protection (Gorilla Glass Victus+) and a large 6000mAh battery at a price where most rivals cut corners on both.

    The trade-off: Exynos 1380 chipset lags behind newer MediaTek chips from rivals in sustained gaming performance.

  3. 3
    Vivo T3 5G
    ₹14,999 · Trust Score 72/100

    Vivo T3 5G offers a bright AMOLED display and dependable everyday performance from the Dimensity 7200 chipset at a genuinely competitive price.

    The trade-off: Some users report noticeable heating during extended charging or heavier gaming sessions.

  4. 4
    Vivo T4x 5G
    ₹13,899 · Trust Score 70/100

    T4x leads with the largest battery in its price segment — 6,500mAh — backed by MIL-STD-810H military certification, making it a dependable budget daily driver.

    The trade-off: No 3.5mm headphone jack, a feature some budget buyers specifically expect at this price.

  5. 5
    Realme Narzo 80x 5G
    ₹13,999 · Trust Score 68/100

    Narzo 80x undercuts most 5G rivals on price while still including a bright 950-nit display and genuine IP68/IP69 military-grade durability — rare combination this low.

    The trade-off: MediaTek Dimensity 6400 chipset is fine for everyday tasks but not built for demanding games.

The verdict

The standard Note 15 trims the Pro+'s camera and charging speed but keeps a genuinely long-lasting battery, positioning it as the value pick in Xiaomi's latest Note lineup. It's the right choice if raw battery endurance matters more to you than camera resolution or charging speed.

What holds up

  • Battery life clearly outlasts the pricier Pro+ model in real-world comparisons, per reviewer testing
  • Noticeably lighter than the Pro+ variant, making it more comfortable for one-handed use
  • Priced meaningfully below the Pro+ while keeping the same core AMOLED display quality

Where it falls short

  • Camera resolution and charging speed both trail the Pro+ and Pro variants in the same lineup
  • Processor benchmarks show it as the slowest in the Note 15 family, per comparison data

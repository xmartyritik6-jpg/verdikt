The verdict

13R brings genuine near-flagship speed — Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, a bright LTPO AMOLED display, and 80W charging — while skipping extras like wireless charging to hit a lower price. Some users report network stability issues after a software update, and camera performance draws mixed reviews at this price, so it's worth checking current firmware notes before buying.

What holds up

  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 delivers genuinely near-flagship performance for meaningfully less than OnePlus' full flagship price
  • 120Hz LTPO AMOLED display with 4500 nits peak brightness stays crisp and dims efficiently for battery savings
  • 4 promised Android upgrades and 6 years of security patches is strong long-term software support

Where it falls short

  • Some users report network connectivity issues (dropping to airplane mode, requiring restarts) after a recent software update
  • Skips wireless charging despite the premium positioning, unlike some similarly priced rivals

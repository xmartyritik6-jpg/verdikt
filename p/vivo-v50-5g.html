The verdict

V50 leans on its ZEISS-branded 50MP dual camera system and a genuinely large curved AMOLED display, positioned as a camera-first mid-ranger under ₹40,000. A GSMArena reader's critique still stands: the lens aperture hasn't improved much generation over generation, so low-light shots trail true camera-first rivals.

What holds up

  • ZEISS-branded 50MP main and 50MP ultra-wide cameras with OIS give genuinely more reliable low-shake shots than typical mid-range dual setups
  • 6.77-inch curved AMOLED display feels premium at this price, with smooth 120Hz scrolling
  • IP68/IP69 rating plus a 6000mAh silicon-carbon battery balance durability and endurance well

Where it falls short

  • Lens aperture has stayed largely unchanged across recent V-series generations, per user feedback, limiting low-light improvement
  • Only 3 years of promised OS updates trails some rivals now offering 4+ years

The verdict

The Galaxy S24 FE brings flagship-adjacent specs — a bright 120Hz display, seven years of promised updates, and IP68 protection — at a price that has dropped significantly from launch. Power users will notice the non-LTPO panel and low-light camera performance lag behind the standard Galaxy S24.

What holds up

  • Seven years of promised OS and security updates is among the longest support in the Android world
  • IP68 rating and Gorilla Glass build give real everyday durability against drops and water
  • 120Hz AMOLED display stays bright and clear even in direct sunlight

Where it falls short

  • Non-LTPO panel means the display can't drop refresh rate as aggressively, costing some battery efficiency
  • Low-light photography noticeably trails the standard Galaxy S24 and newer flagships

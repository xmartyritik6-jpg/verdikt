The verdict

The base iPhone 17 finally gets features that used to be Pro-only — a 120Hz ProMotion display and the same A19 chip as the Pro models — making it the most sensible iPhone to buy this year. The trade-off is Apple's usual premium pricing; you're paying flagship money even for the 'basic' model.

What holds up

  • 120Hz ProMotion OLED display and the A19 chip bring Pro-level performance to the standard model for the first time
  • Up to 30 hours of video playback gives genuinely long battery life for a compact flagship
  • 48MP dual camera setup with a wide and ultra-wide lens covers most everyday shooting needs well

Where it falls short

  • Starting price of ₹82,900 puts it well above budget and even most mid-range Android flagships
  • Only 8GB RAM means next-gen on-device AI features in future iOS updates may be limited on this base model

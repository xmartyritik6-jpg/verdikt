The verdict

Reno 14 Pro steps up the design and camera experience over the standard Reno 14, with reviewers praising its display sharpness and AI feature set. It's not without flaws — reviewers note some rough edges in day-to-day polish despite the premium positioning, so it's more a 'mostly there' flagship-adjacent phone than a fully finished one.

What holds up

  • Sharp, bright display draws consistent praise in hands-on reviews for everyday visibility
  • Extensive AI feature set adds genuinely useful shortcuts for photo editing and search
  • Premium design language feels like a clear step up from the standard Reno 14

Where it falls short

  • Reviewers note the phone tries to balance too many things at once, leaving some features feeling not fully polished
  • Commands a real price premium over the standard Reno 14 for incremental, not transformative, upgrades

The verdict

Nord 6 leads with a genuinely massive 9,000mAh battery — unusual even by 2026 standards — paired with a fast 165Hz display and comprehensive IP66/68/69/69K durability. Frequent price hikes since launch (three rounds reported) mean the 'value' positioning has eroded somewhat, so check the current price carefully before assuming it's still a bargain.

What holds up

  • 9,000mAh battery is genuinely massive, comfortably covering 1.5-2 days of heavy use without a mid-day charge
  • IP66, IP68, IP69, and IP69K ratings together give unusually comprehensive dust and water protection
  • 165Hz AMOLED display with 3600 nits peak brightness is near flagship-tier for this segment

Where it falls short

  • Price has been hiked three times since launch per reported coverage, eroding its original value-for-money positioning
  • At 217g, the large battery adds noticeable heft compared to lighter rivals in the same price bracket

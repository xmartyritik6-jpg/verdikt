The verdict

Narzo 80x undercuts most 5G rivals on price while still including a bright 950-nit display and genuine IP68/IP69 military-grade durability — rare combination this low. It's a straightforward budget pick built for students and first-time 5G buyers rather than power users.

What holds up

  • IP68/IP69 rating plus military-grade shock resistance is unusually rugged for a phone under ₹14,000
  • 950-nit peak brightness with a 10-layer eye comfort system makes the display comfortable even in bright daylight
  • Ships with Android 15 out of the box — ahead of many pricier rivals still on older software at launch

Where it falls short

  • MediaTek Dimensity 6400 chipset is fine for everyday tasks but not built for demanding games
  • 2MP macro secondary camera adds little real photography value beyond the main 50MP sensor

The verdict

Redmi Note 14 5G brings a genuinely bright, smooth 120Hz AMOLED display and a capable Dimensity 7025-Ultra chipset that handles daily multitasking and casual gaming without fuss. Its IP64 rating protects against splashes but isn't built for anything beyond light rain, so treat it as everyday protection rather than real water resistance.

What holds up

  • 120Hz AMOLED display hits up to 2100 nits, staying easily visible even in bright outdoor sunlight
  • The 5110mAh battery with 45W charging comfortably covers a full day with fast top-ups when needed
  • Four years of promised security updates is unusually long support for this price segment

Where it falls short

  • IP64 rating only covers light splashes, not submersion — don't treat it as fully water-resistant
  • Some variants ship without expandable storage, locking you into whatever configuration you buy

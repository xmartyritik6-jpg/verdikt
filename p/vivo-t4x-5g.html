The verdict

T4x leads with the largest battery in its price segment — 6,500mAh — backed by MIL-STD-810H military certification, making it a dependable budget daily driver. A recurring user complaint: no 3.5mm headphone jack and no expandable storage, which some budget buyers specifically look for at this price.

What holds up

  • 6,500mAh battery is genuinely the largest in its price segment, comfortably lasting a full day of heavy use
  • MIL-STD-810H military-grade certification plus IP64 rating gives real everyday drop and splash protection
  • 44W fast charging is a meaningful convenience given the oversized battery

Where it falls short

  • No 3.5mm headphone jack, a feature some budget buyers specifically expect at this price
  • No expandable storage slot, unlike some competitors that still offer a microSD option

The verdict

Note 15 Pro+ brings a 200MP main camera and genuinely rugged IP66/68/69 protection (reviewers cite it surviving ice-submersion and drop tests) to the upper-mid-range. The most common critique across reviews: no telephoto lens despite the price, and middling raw performance compared to what the spec sheet promises.

What holds up

  • IP66, IP68, and IP69 ratings together give genuinely rare, comprehensive durability — reviewers specifically note it surviving extreme drop and ice-submersion tests
  • 200MP main camera captures realistic, detailed shots that reviewers rate as a highlight of the phone
  • 6,500mAh battery with 100W charging comfortably covers a full day even with heavy use

Where it falls short

  • No telephoto lens despite the price, a recurring complaint across multiple reviews
  • Reviewers note middling raw performance and some software bloat despite the premium camera focus

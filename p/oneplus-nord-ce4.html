The verdict

The Nord CE4 pairs a Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 chipset with genuinely fast 100W charging, going from empty to full in well under 30 minutes — a rare combination at this price. Camera performance in low light is where it falls behind pricier OnePlus siblings, with visible noise creeping in past dusk.

What holds up

  • 100W SUPERVOOC charging takes the 5500mAh battery from empty to full in around 27 minutes
  • Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 handles multitasking and moderate gaming smoothly without noticeable lag
  • AMOLED display with 120Hz refresh rate feels fluid for everyday scrolling and video

Where it falls short

  • Low-light photos show visible noise and softness compared to pricier OnePlus models
  • IP54 rating only protects against light splashes, not submersion or heavy rain exposure
